This Contract ("Agreement") is entered into on 2026-03-01 by and between:

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of
twelve (12) months, unless earlier terminated under Section 6. The Agreement shall

Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Expiry Date: 2027-02-28

Client shall pay Vendor within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice,
in accordance with the rates set out in Schedule B.

during the term of this Agreement.

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon thirty (30) days written
notice if the other party materially breaches any term herein.
